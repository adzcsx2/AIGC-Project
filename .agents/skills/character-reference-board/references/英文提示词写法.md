# 英文提示词写法

先看真实输入，填写具体身份与动作，不发送未替换占位符。中文需求自动译为英文；说明和文件名中文。
原中文模板保留在重组前备份。以下是写法模块，按实际身体结构和工具调整，不机械套用。

## 身份与版式

> Create one multi-view character and motion reference board of the same character shown in the reference image. Preserve the observed facial features, hairstyle, accessories, costume layers, colors, materials, proportions and asymmetric markings. Extend unseen rear details conservatively from the visible construction. Maintain the reference's visual style.
>
> Use a neutral gray background and soft, clear lighting. Place front, back and strict 90-degree side full-body views in the upper row. Place a front facial close-up, a matching strict side facial close-up, and a wider panel showing the character's actual movement posture in the lower row. Show the character's left side with the nose pointing to screen left in both side views. Keep asymmetric features on their correct anatomical side. Show the entire body and its extremities with breathing room in each body panel; use close framing only in the facial panels. All panels show the same individual from distinct views. Keep silhouettes separated. No merged limbs, cross-panel appendages, motion ghosts, labels, text or watermarks.

实际交付时补入本次角色的具体身份和运动姿态；静态用途不添加无关动作格。封闭机甲面甲不虚构内部人脸。

## 蛇妖运动

> In every body panel, retain one human head, one upper torso, two arms, and exactly one continuous serpentine lower body tapering to one closed tail tip. No human legs, feet, tail forks or additional snake heads. Keep the waist-to-abdomen connection visible and the scale patterns continuous. In the motion panel, use a low forward crawling posture viewed from a front-side angle: the upper body leans low and forward, the arms bend naturally for support, and the broad ventral scales contact the gray ground. The single tail follows behind in a readable gentle curve with its sole tip fully visible. Show grounded serpentine locomotion, not an upright standing pose sliding sideways or human running.

根据实际角色补配色与身体特征，不继承历史蛇妖服装；也不是所有运动角色都无腿。

## 机甲飞行

> Preserve the reference mech's head, armor divisions, joints and original limb count. Use only propulsion structures present in the reference or approved design. In the motion panel, show the intended airborne posture with visible ground clearance, a clearly readable body pitch, and arms and legs arranged for the intended flight phase. Keep wings and thrusters attached in their correct locations and orientations. Use restrained propulsion effects only to clarify direction, leaving the body readable. Do not invent extra wings, tails, nozzles or weapons.

交付时将飞行相位、俯仰和四肢姿态写成具体动作，不能只写intended posture。

## 局部修正与视频绑定

编辑明确指出哪格哪个错误、期望连接关系，锁住合格格的身份、造型、光线和排布；修正后检查全板。
交给允许prompt的视频入口时，可按真实图序采用：

> All views on this board define one character's identity, costume, anatomy and movement form. Show only one instance of this character in the scene. Do not reproduce the gray background, panel borders or multiple views. Follow the audited motion source for action timing, trajectories, contact and camera work, and the separate scene reference for environment and lighting.

原创没有源片时，最后一句改成实际确认的动作设计。不给不支持prompt的入口新增字段。
