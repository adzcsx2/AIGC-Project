# 英文提示词写法

先读本作品style与人物YAML，实看身份图和 [三视图版式](版式与三视图.md)。中文需求自动译为英文；说明和文件名中文。以下为已按三视图规则复核的写法模块，不是某个作品可直接提交的最终提示词：每次使用仍须填入已观察的具体身份、服装、结构、自然表情及本片英文风格约束，绑定真实参考后完成二次审计闭环。不发送占位符，不把示例人物或画风当身份依据。

## 身份与版式

无原图的新角色，先把模块中的identity reference等图像依据改为已确认设定的具体英文描述，不声称存在未提供的参考图；有图时区分身份、项目风格和排版参考。动物须同时替换人类脸部、手、鞋、衣服和双足站姿等不适用条款，保留其实际头部、毛发/鳞片、四肢/尾翼与支撑结构；仅省略正面身体的头部，不能为了套模块拟人化或增加视图。

> Create one character reference board containing exactly three views of the same individual: one straight-on front portrait close-up, one straight-on front body view with the head omitted, and one complete rear full-body view. Preserve the character identity and costume defined by the identity reference, including facial features, hairstyle, accessories, costume layers, colors, proportions and asymmetric markings. Extend unseen rear details conservatively from the visible construction. Apply the specified project visual style, material treatment and rendering consistently across all three views. Treat any supplied layout example only as a composition guide; do not copy its character, costume or visual style.
>
> Use a single 16:9 landscape canvas with a neutral gray background and soft, even studio illumination. On the left, show the front-facing body from the neck or collar line down to the soles, omitting only the head in this view. Keep both hands, the entire outfit, lower body and footwear visible. The upper boundary is a clean, non-graphic costume-reference presentation, with no wound or detached head. In the center, show the same character facing directly away from the camera, including the complete back of the head, hair, body and footwear down to the soles; do not turn the head toward the camera. Match the two body views in anatomical scale and foot baseline; the front body view is naturally shorter because its head is omitted, not because the body is resized or stretched. On the right, place a large straight-on front portrait showing the full head, facial features, hair, accessories, shoulders and upper chest. Keep the face centered and readable with a natural expression, without a three-quarter head turn. The portrait occupies roughly half the canvas and does not overlap the two body views.
>
> Keep a clear, anatomically natural display pose and preserve asymmetric details on their correct anatomical sides. Show grounded support and soft contact shadows beneath the feet; prevent hands from intersecting clothing. Leave room around the body silhouettes and all original extremities. The omitted head belongs only to the left body presentation; retain the head in the rear view and the portrait. Include exactly these three views, with no side view, profile face, elevated or low-angle view, action pose panel, expression panel, extra inset, duplicate figure, motion ghost, labels, text or watermark.

16:9是本版式的默认值；用户另定画幅时改正文并检查三视图仍可读，不向不支持画幅字段的工具编造参数。自然表情按已确认人物具体化，仍保持身份可辨认，不新增情绪表演合集。

## 非人结构适配

蛇妖等无腿角色必须替换上文feet/footwear/soles和站姿条款，不把人形模块与无腿约束同时保留；按原有身体写下部末端与支撑，例如：

> In both body views, preserve the original upper torso, two arms and one continuous serpentine lower body tapering to a single closed tail tip. Show the entire lower body and sole tail tip clearly, with the broad ventral surface supporting the body on the gray ground and a corresponding contact shadow. No human legs, feet, tail forks or extra snake heads. Omit the head only from the front body presentation; retain the complete rear head in the rear view and the original head in the front portrait. Do not add a crawling panel or any other view.

保留本角色的实际配色、鳞片与服装，不继承历史蛇妖设定。封闭机甲的正面特写展示原有头部/面甲，不虚构内部人脸；保留已有肢体与部件，不为展示飞行新增翼、喷口或飞行格。其他结构按实图改写，不将蛇妖模块泛用于所有角色。

不要笼统写“每个身体视图都保留一颗头”或“所有全身格头脚完整”，这些句子会与正面无头展示冲突；也不要用全图禁止头部的负面词。

## 局部修正与视频绑定

编辑明确指出哪个视图的哪个错误、期望范围或连接关系，锁住其他合格视图的身份、造型、光线和排布；修正后检查全板始终只有三视图，不把修补结果另排成第四格。
交给允许prompt的视频入口时，可按真实图序采用：

> The three views on this board define one character's identity, costume and anatomy. Show only one instance of this character in the scene, with the complete original head and body. The omission of the head in the front body view is only a reference-sheet presentation choice; never reproduce that omission in the video. Do not reproduce the gray background, layout or multiple views. Follow the audited motion source for action timing, trajectories, contact and camera work, and the separate scene reference for environment and lighting. Apply the specified project visual style throughout the entire clip.

这只是绑定模块：实际视频正文仍须填入本片英文风格核心和已确认动作/表演/声音等适用要求；原创没有源片时把motion source改成具体已确认动作设计。不给不支持prompt的入口新增字段，也不为本规则更新发起视频任务。
